About CardioSight & Project Authors

Capstone Project under the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCOPE), VIT-AP University.

Research Project Abstract

Multi-label cardiac arrhythmia detection using deep neural networks typically requires massive, centralized ECG datasets that compromise patient data privacy. We present CardioSight (ECG-FL-XAI), an uncertainty-aware, explainable federated learning framework trained across 5 geographically distributed clinical cohorts from the PhysioNet Challenge 2021.

Utilizing a 1D ResNet-34 backbone with adaptive federated optimizers (FedAvg, FedProx, FedAdam), we demonstrate that predictive uncertainty computed via Monte Carlo Dropout serves as a rigorous, verifiable safety metric correlated with inter-hospital explanation consistency (Grad-CAM and SHAP).
